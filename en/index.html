This Bluesky Video Downloader is a lightweight tool for videos attached to public Bluesky posts. It does not require a Bluesky login or a separate application.

FAQ

How do I download a Bluesky video?

Copy the link to a public post containing a video, paste it above, and press Download Video.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. You can use Safari and find the downloaded file in the Files app.

Do I need to sign in to Bluesky?

No. Public posts can be processed without signing in.

Why can't it find a video?

The post may be private, deleted, contain no video, or you may have pasted a profile URL instead of a post URL.
